# Voice

Lab talks like a calm lab bench: flasks, jars, notes, results. It's dry and
never zany. The facts always come first.

## Personality

Settings › Appearance › Personality has two levels.

- **Default** is the shipped voice. Greetings, waits, and empty states may add a
  line of character, and rare moments get a small flourish.
- **Quiet** shows the plain copy and turns off every optional flourish: the
  greeting assemble, plan confetti, and anything added later.

Read the setting with `useQuiet()` from `src/lib/voice.ts`. Gate every new
flourish and every line of character on it.

## Six rules

1. **Facts first.** A line may add character. It never replaces the information.
2. **One quip per surface.** A screen gets one voice moment, never two.
3. **Lab-bench register.** Dry, never zany. No “oops”, no exclamation marks, no
   emoji.
4. **Plain where it counts.** Approvals, billing, deletes, and anything with
   money or files stay literal. An error says “Couldn’t …” and what happens
   next.
5. **Plainer the more you see it.** Every send is silent. Every visit is mild.
   Once-ever moments get the jokes.
6. **Seeded, never rerolled.** A line is picked from the date and its context,
   so a refresh keeps it.
